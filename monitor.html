Analog Discomfort-Index Monitor

Analog circuits · PCB designMar–Jun 2024

Team course project · Basic Circuit Theory and Laboratory

Contributed to an analog discomfort-index system that processes temperature and humidity signals using op-amps, diodes, and resistors. The project used the computed index to select a control voltage for a small fan.

My contribution: Custom footprint creation and PCB layout in KiCad, as documented in the team’s interim report.

Core idea: compute the index directly with analog circuits

Log/antilog circuitry forms the product of the temperature and humidity signals. Weighted op-amp summation combines that product with the individual sensor terms, and comparators translate the resulting index into discrete control levels.

VDI = 0.5 VRH VT − 0.93 VRH + 1.39 VT

Voltage-domain expression used in the report. VT and VRH denote the temperature and humidity sensor voltages.

Intermediate validation

With VRH fixed at 4 V and VT swept from 1.9 to 2.3 V, LTspice produced stepped control outputs of approximately 3.47, 4.53, and 5.00 V. Breadboard work also revealed clipping near −4.1 V in the multiplier output; the report identified op-amp output swing as a likely cause and proposed further supply and input-range checks.

My PCB design work

Created custom footprints and routed the KiCad PCB from the team’s schematic. The interim design included footprints for the ETH-01DV sensor and LM358 devices, with a 3D model used to inspect the board layout.
